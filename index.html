Got questions?
Here's what
we hear most.

Do you really offer a 10-day free trial with no obligation?
Yes. Rising Tide is the only gym in the area with a full 10-day trial and absolutely zero obligation. Full facility access, classes, kids care, and staff support — everything you need to know if we're a fit before you commit.
I've never worked out before. Can I still start?
Absolutely. Most of our members start exactly where you are. Every workout is universally scaled — a different version for every level — and coaches walk you through form step by step.
How quickly will I see results?
Consistency is the key. We recommend three classes per week to start. Most members feel measurable change in the first 30 days, and see it soon after.
What does a class actually look like?
Sixty minutes, led by a qualified coach. We warm up together, brief the workout and demo proper form, work through the session with coaching cues, and cool down together to accelerate recovery.
Do you help with nutrition and recovery too?
Yes — meal planning, supplement guidance, sleep and recovery routines. Training is only part of the equation, and we treat it that way.
How do I get started?
Click Start My 10-Day Trial above or call 910·400·1157. You'll book a free 15-minute intro, tour the gym, and meet a coach. From there we start you on the trial.
